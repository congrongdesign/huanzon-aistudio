# Motif

Source: https://github.com/SweetRetry/motif
Registry: https://motif.sweetretry.com/

Message, Attachment, Strength Slider, Cover Fan, Model Select, Resolution Picker, Generation Overlay and Flow Edge are copied and adapted from the Motif registry. Adaptations include Chinese labels, provider-bound model IDs, bounded catalogue rendering, original/preview image decoding, task identity and canvas lineage geometry. The bottom canvas composer and settings use Motif interaction patterns. Generation and edge effects use the Motion runtime under its MIT license.
